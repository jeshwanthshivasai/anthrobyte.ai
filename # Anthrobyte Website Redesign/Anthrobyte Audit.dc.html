§ 01 · EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The copy is the asset. The visuals are the liability.

Anthrobyte is an enterprise AI company (founded 2025; Minneapolis HQ with a registered entity in Hyderabad; one funding round; CEO Sunayana Pulusani — via Crunchbase/Tracxn). It deploys modular supply-chain AI agents under the product name AATLAS — pricing, procurement, inventory, supplier risk, compliance, BoM — positioned as "Structured Intelligence": governance-first, human-centred, clarity before acceleration.

The site's verbal identity is genuinely distinct: "Complexity Is Layered." · "Clarity must precede deployment." · "We treat AI as an influence layer within complex systems." Every one of those lines is a spatial, dimensional, systemic idea. And every one of them is illustrated with a flat static SVG inside a stock template pattern — hero / three steps / tab cards / FAQ / banner.

The redesign doesn't need a new concept. The concept is already in their words — layers, ripple effects, alignment, networks. It has simply never been rendered. Our job is to build the site their copy already describes.

One more piece of ammo for an unsolicited pitch: their careers page is hiring a ReactJS frontend developer "to craft stunning UI" — the appetite for a premium front-end already exists inside the company.

§ 03 · SOURCE-LEVEL FINDINGS — THE NITTY GRITTY

Ten findings from the shipped markup

  1. Framework: Next.js. The next-size-adjust meta tag and a dynamic /og image route on every page are Next.js fingerprints. SSR/SSG, canonicals set correctly per page.
  2. Imagery is 100% static SVG served from a flat public folder: anthrobyte_logo.svg · hero/Layered_Complexity_Anthrobyte.svg · homeCircle.svg · footer-anthrobyte.svg · journalicon.svg · blog-forward-arrow.svg · forward-white-arrow.svg · Copyright.svg. No photography, no video, no canvas, no 3D — anywhere.
  3. The DOM ships responsive duplicates as real markup. Nav appears twice; Home's 3-step "How We Work" block appears three times; the agent card deck twice; AI Transformation's maturity ladder and "The Pattern" poll twice each. Cost: page weight, screen readers reading content twice, triple maintenance.
  4. Duplicate live routes: /aatlas-agents and /atlas-agents are both indexed with near-identical meta ("AAtlas" vs "Atlas"). SEO cannibalization plus naming drift — the brand is spelled AAtlas, AATLAS, and Aatlas across pages.
  5. Alt text is keyword-stuffed, e.g. "Supplier risk intelligence icon for disruption signal detection" — written for crawlers, not people.
  6. The © glyph is an image. The footer loads Copyright.svg to render a copyright symbol — a classic template tell.
  7. The highest-intent CTA exits the brand. Every "Let's Talk" jumps to bookings.cloud.microsoft/…?ismsaljsauthenabled — a raw Microsoft Bookings URL at the exact moment of conversion.
  8. Dead SEO practice: a keywords meta on every page; one shared OG image sitewide, so all four pages preview identically when shared.
  9. FAQ dump on the homepage: 8 accordion questions occupying prime real estate before the closing CTA.
  10. Legacy positioning still live: /knowledge carries the old tagline ("build AI that serves — guided by empathy…"), coexisting with the new "Structured Intelligence" voice.

§ 04 · PAGE-BY-PAGE TEARDOWN

Four pages, section by section

4.1 — Home /

MAP: hero → "The Challenge" (layered-complexity SVG) → How We Work (3 steps) → Why Anthrobyte (5-item approach) → agent deck (5 tabbed cards + metrics) → FAQ ×8 → CTA banner → footer

KEEP   The headline system ("Structured Intelligence for Complex Operations"), the 3-stage method, real per-agent outcome metrics, the closing line "If you're seeking clarity before acceleration, we'd be glad to think with you."

FAILS  Zero scroll choreography; the "layered complexity" thesis rendered as one static SVG; the agent deck is a default tab component; FAQ occupies the emotional climax; every section is a recognizable stock pattern.

HOOKS  "Complexity is layered" is a literal z-axis scene begging for WebGL. The 3 stages want a pinned scroll sequence. The five metrics (50%, ~10%, 5%, 3–7%, 60–70%) want kinetic number treatments. Agents are network nodes.

4.2 — AAtlas Agents /aatlas-agents

MAP: acronym hero → agent deck (same 5 cards as Home) → Intelligence-as-a-Service → ALIGNMENT (5 commitments) → DEPLOYMENT (4 steps) → CTA → footer

KEEP   The acronym itself — Anthrobyte Agentic Transformation Layer for Adaptive Supply Chains — plus the IaaS subscription framing ("without replacing existing enterprise systems") and the 5 alignment commitments (ERP-integrated, transparent boundaries, human-in-the-loop, audit-friendly, decision ownership).

FAILS  Roughly 60% of the page duplicates Home, so the flagship product page has the weakest reason to exist. The product is literally named a Layer and no visual ever shows a layer entering a stack.

HOOKS  Hero where the acronym assembles letter by letter. An isometric ERP stack with the AATLAS layer sliding in between systems. A per-agent orbital/graph selector replacing tabs. The 4 deployment steps as a pinned progress rail.

4.3 — AI Transformation /ai-transformation

MAP: stat hero (McKinsey 88%) → "The Pattern" poll question → 8 conditions grid → 5-stage maturity ladder with two markers → assessment duo (scorecard vs founder call) → footer

KEEP   Strongest content page on the site. The confrontational poll question is genuinely good. The maturity ladder's two markers — "Where most actually are" vs "Where most leaders think they are" — and the line "The gap between where you think you are and where you are, that's the work."

FAILS  The curve isn't a curve — it's stacked text lists (duplicated in the DOM). The 8 conditions render as a plain grid. The poll appears inert.

HOOKS  A scroll-driven maturity curve that draws itself, with the two org markers visibly separating — the gap IS the story. The 8 conditions as a sequential reveal. Make the poll actually respond and hand off into the assessment.

4.4 — Journal /anthrobyte-journal

MAP: eyebrow "WHERE PERSPECTIVE PRECEDES IMPLEMENTATION" → title + icon → featured essay → category filter (6 tabs) → 3 essay cards → CTA → footer

KEEP   Real essays with literary spine — the featured piece "Intelligence Requires Direction" is built on Asimov's Runaround: "Technology accelerates whatever structure already exists. If incentives are misaligned, automation intensifies tension."

FAILS  Heavy 6-tab filter UI governing exactly four essays. Generic blog-index card layout. Category casing drifts ("Alignment & Governance" vs "Alignment and Governance").

HOOKS  Treat it as an actual journal: big serif, folio numbers, reading time, essay-first hierarchy. This page can be the quiet typographic counterpoint that proves the system has range.

§ 06 · DIAGNOSIS

Seven reasons it reads as a template

  1. Every section is a stock pattern: hero / 3-steps / tabs / accordion FAQ / banner. Nothing is shaped by the content it carries.
  2. Illustrations are one-off diagram clipart — no shared geometry, no motif, no system.
  3. Zero motion identity. A company selling "adaptive, sensing, real-time" systems has a completely inert site.
  4. The product page duplicates the homepage; the two strongest pages compete instead of compounding.
  5. Conversion exits the brand into a Microsoft Bookings URL.
  6. No typographic drama — everything sits in the same mid-size band; nothing is allowed to be huge or small.
  7. Craft drift everywhere: AAtlas/AATLAS/Aatlas, duplicate routes, © as an image, stuffed alt text. Details are where "premium" lives, and the details leak.

§ 08 · THREE DIRECTION SEEDS — FOR THE BRAINSTORM

All three render their own language back at them

Not final concepts — starting points to react to, and they mix (B's map can live inside A's dark field).

SEED A

The Stratified Field

"Complexity is layered" made literal: a persistent WebGL stack of translucent strata — leadership intent above, operations mid, ground reality below. Scroll descends the camera through the layers; agents are lights tracing paths between them.

FEEL — dark instrument, one hot accent
WEBGL — one persistent scene, section-driven
ISO SVG — agent schematics, deployment rails
RISK — "dark AI site" cliché; counter with editorial serif + restraint

SEED B

The Living Atlas

AATLAS as cartography. An isometric supply-chain territory — ports, routes, warehouses, customs — drawn as a clean 2D iso SVG kit that pans and reveals with scroll. Agents are beacons switching on across the map. WebGL reserved for a hero globe/network with arcing routes.

FEEL — light, technical-optimistic, paper + ink
WEBGL — hero globe/network only
ISO SVG — the whole world: modular map kit
RISK — illustration volume; needs a tight reusable kit

SEED C

The Decision Blueprint

Governance rendered as engineering documentation: blueprint grid, monospace annotation, numbered figures (FIG 01…), schematics that draw themselves via stroke animation. One signature WebGL object — a rotating layered core — recurs on every page.

FEEL — precise, audit-coded, quiet confidence
WEBGL — one signature object, reused
ISO SVG — self-drawing schematics everywhere
RISK — can read cold; warm with serif voice + micro-motion
